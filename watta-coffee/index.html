Our story

Every sip
worth coming
back for.

At WATTA Coffee, we're all about good drinks and better days. We're a Bengaluru-based beverage brand bringing together specialty coffee, ceremonial-grade matcha, and ube, crafted into refreshing iced drinks with quality ingredients and a whole lot of love.

From our little street setup at HSR 2.0 to office pop-ups, corporate events, and collaborations, we're on a mission to make every sip worth coming back for.
